01 — Company Introduction

Business support built around people and technology.

Panchal Technology is a BPO/contact-center company specializing in customer care, sales and business support services.

Panchal Technology is a nearshore, bi-lingual contact center specializing in customer care and sales while also offering a wide variety of business support services.

Our workforce is trained and experienced, supported by technology designed to suit different operating requirements.

We are based in Karnal, Haryana, India and provide leads, sales, support and customer service according to client requirements.
